Research date: 25 November 2026. Market: India. Language: English.
This is replacement copy and a publishing brief, not a deployed website change.

## Editorial decisions

Use ChametApps.com as the brand throughout. The owner confirms the agency is certified; the certification wording below relies on that confirmation, not independent certification verification. Do not invent an agency certificate number or display an official-platform ownership badge.

Keep the existing /host.html and /agency.html URLs. The new paths proposed below require a check for existing equivalents before implementation. Do not rename established pages solely to insert keywords. Preserve existing contact/blog URLs and useful content. No agency-changing guide is proposed.

### What the competitors already cover

- chamet.agency has dashboard explanations and a substantial help taxonomy covering registration, commissions, wallet linking and payment problems. These are established topics, not an untouched content gap.
- chametagency.live separates the Chamet-to-wallet and wallet-to-payment-account stages, includes screenshots, and discusses method availability and fees.
- rchametagency.com has a short pending/failed withdrawal checklist. Its opening paragraph groups pending and failed transactions together; a clearer status-specific decision table is a useful improvement.
- chametagency.org, also owned by the user, already has registration and commission guides. ChametApps.com should use its own task-focused copy rather than replicate that site's paragraphs.

### Specific opportunities for ChametApps.com

1. Put agency login near the top, with app-login versus agency-login clarification and the actual destination domain visible.
2. Use the six prepared image sequences with written instructions and a clear expected result after each stage.
3. Distinguish registration, verification, wallet authorization, withdrawal submission and payment receipt. These are different milestones.
4. Provide separate checks for a missing button, an unlinked wallet, a pending transaction, a failed transaction and a completed transaction not yet received.
5. Explain dollar balances versus actual rupee receipts without promising a fixed INR conversion.
6. Keep the reader's next action beside each guide: registration → dashboard/profile → wallet linking → cash out.

These are editorial opportunities identified from the reviewed pages, not claims that no competitor covers any of them.

### Corrections needed on the current site

The homepage currently includes a top-platform claim, delay-free weekly payment wording, monthly income estimates and a commission table reaching 30%. The agency page describes app “Agent mode” and a 24–48 hour approval process. The host page lists payment methods and time-to-earn promises. Those claims were not substantiated by the sources and screenshots reviewed for this rewrite. Replace them with the concrete steps below. Do not carry them into metadata or promotional cards.

The official host-earnings page distinguishes withdrawable balances and cash-out methods. Another official help overview uses weekly settlement wording. Because settlement and withdrawal can refer to different stages, this copy directs readers to their displayed withdrawable balance instead of promising one universal weekly payout schedule.

Use a visible step counter, swipe, previous/next and tap-to-enlarge. Also render all written steps as normal page text. Do not place essential instructions only inside images. Use the main folders, not archived images. Add one note: “Images are annotated recreations of example screens. Labels and account requirements may vary.”

# How to Withdraw From MetWallet

This guide starts inside MetWallet. Check your available balance and follow the account requirements displayed before choosing a withdrawal method.

## Eight Cash-Out Steps

1. Open MetWallet and review your balance.
2. Open Basic Level where shown, complete the requested details and submit them. Complete additional verification if your account requires it.
3. Return to the homepage and select Cash Out when available.
4. Choose a listed withdrawal method and bind your own destination account. Review the details carefully.
5. If prompted, create your payment password and enter it again to confirm. This setup step may appear at a different point in your account.
6. Select your destination and enter the amount. Read the displayed minimum, fee and payout quote.
7. Submit the request and enter your payment password when prompted.
8. Read the confirmation and estimated arrival time. Save the transaction reference and check the destination account for receipt.

Use the payout quote provided for your selected method. A dollar wallet balance and the final rupee credit can differ because of currency conversion and fees. This guide does not promise a fixed USD-to-INR rate.

## How Long Will It Take?
